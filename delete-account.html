Delete Your Account – Jisnu Employee Portal

If you would like to request deletion of your account and associated data from the Jisnu Employee Portal mobile application, please follow the steps below.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email request to info.jdsolutions2018@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered email address used in the app

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account information (name, email)
  • Employee profile data
  • Reports submitted by the user
  • Requests created by the user

Data That May Be Retained

Some administrative or attendance records may be retained for internal organizational compliance for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

For any questions regarding account deletion, contact: info.jdsolutions2018@gmail.com
